## ORC License (remastered mechanics)

The remastered rules (Player Core, Monster Core, and later books) publish
their game mechanics under the
[ORC License](https://paizo.com/orclicense). Content packs in
`libs/content/*` declare their licence in their manifest
(`ContentLicense.Orc`, `PaizoCommunityUse` or `Homebrew`), and only transcribe
the mechanical fields the SDK models (statistics, traits), not rules prose.

## Contributor checklist for content packs

1. Declare the licence in the pack manifest.
2. Transcribe mechanics only; link to the source rather than copying prose.
3. No Paizo art, logos or trade dress.
4. Never put Paizo content behind anything that costs money or requires sign-up.
